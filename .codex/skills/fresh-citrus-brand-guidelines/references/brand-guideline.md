Design a modern AI Agent nutrition/wellness website and app UI based strictly on the following brand guideline.

The brand direction is:

“Fresh Citrus AI Wellness”

Fresh • Energetic • Youthful • Healthy • Friendly • Nutritious • Trustworthy • Intelligent

The product is an AI-powered nutrition and wellness agent. It helps users create healthy meal plans, smoothie recipes, nutrition plans, calorie/macro guidance, ingredient-based suggestions, and personalized wellness recommendations.

This is not just a normal nutrition website.
This is not just a landing page.
This is not just a chatbot.

It must feel like a trustworthy AI Agent workspace where users can:

==================================================

1. CORE PRODUCT PRINCIPLES
   ==================================================

The product must follow these AI Agent frontend principles:

1. Clarity over decoration
   Users must always understand what they can do, what the AI is doing, and what happens next.

2. Control over automation
   The AI can suggest freely, but the user must control important actions.

3. Trust over magic
   The AI must not feel like a black box. Show context, assumptions, missing information, and confirmation states when needed.

4. Consistency over novelty
   Use consistent colors, spacing, cards, buttons, states, and interaction patterns.

5. Accessibility over visual tricks
   The UI must be readable, keyboard-friendly, and usable on mobile.

6. Performance over heavy effects
   Use lightweight motion and avoid unnecessary visual complexity.

7. Fresh wellness over clinical healthcare
   The product should feel friendly, delicious, and encouraging, not medical or intimidating.

22. DO / DON’T

DO:

* Use cream background generously
* Use orange for brand energy and important accents
* Use accessible dark orange for important CTA buttons
* Use green to reinforce healthy choices
* Use yellow for tips, energy, and confirmation states
* Use coral carefully for warmth, favorites, or errors
* Use fruit and meal visuals
* Use rounded, soft cards
* Use friendly AI microcopy
* Keep UI airy and delicious
* Make the design feel fresh and easy to use
* Use Playfair Display for emotional landing headlines
* Use Poppins for app readability
* Show AI states clearly
* Give users control over AI actions
* Structure AI outputs into useful cards and sections

DON’T:

* Do not make the UI look like a hospital app
* Do not make it look like a hardcore gym app
* Do not make it look like a generic chatbot
* Do not overuse coral pink
* Do not use lemon yellow for main CTA
* Do not create a dark luxury interface
* Do not make the layout crowded
* Do not use random gradients everywhere
* Do not use harsh black text when deep brown works better
* Do not ignore mobile spacing
* Do not create generic SaaS UI with no food/wellness feeling
* Do not hide AI state from users
* Do not let AI execute important actions without confirmation
* Do not return long raw text when structured output is better
